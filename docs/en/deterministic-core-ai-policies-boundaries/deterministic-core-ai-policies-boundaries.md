# 5. Deterministic Core, Policies, and Boundaries of AI Use

> **Version:** `0.3.1`  
> **Status:** in development

This file establishes the working structure for the chapter. Normative provisions will be formulated and validated separately.

## 5.1. Deterministic Core

### Purpose

Define the stable and verifiable rules that underpin the use of AI in a project.

### Draft structure

- composition of the deterministic core;
- rules subject to automated and manual verification;
- relationship between the core, context, and contracts;
- change control and versioning of core rules.

## 5.2. Policies

### Purpose

Define which policies constrain AI actions and how they are applied, checked, and changed.

### Draft structure

- authority, data, and tool policies;
- conditions for authorizing, prohibiting, and escalating actions;
- recording exceptions and accountable decisions;
- checking policy compliance.

## 5.3. Boundaries of AI Use

### Purpose

Define the boundaries within which AI may participate in work and the conditions that require human involvement.

### Draft structure

- permitted and prohibited task classes;
- trust, technical, and organizational boundaries;
- signs that agreed authority has been exceeded;
- pausing work and handing decisions to a human.
